Example 6. Matrix Multiplication.

Given the matrices
\begin{equation*} A = \left[ \begin{array}{rr} 4 \amp 2 \\ 0 \amp 1 \\ -3 \amp 4 \\ 2 \amp 0 \\ \end{array} \right], \,\,\, B = \left[ \begin{array}{rrr} -2 \amp 3 \amp 0 \\ 1 \amp 2 \amp -2 \\ \end{array} \right] \end{equation*}
we have
\begin{align*} AB = \amp \left[ A\twovec{-2}{1} \,\,\, A\twovec{3}{2} \,\,\, A\twovec{0}{-2}\right]\\ = \amp \left[ \begin{array}{rrr} -6 \amp 16 \amp -4 \\ 1 \amp 2 \amp -2 \\ 10 \amp -1 \amp -8 \\ -4 \amp 6 \amp 0 \\ \end{array} \right]. \end{align*}
