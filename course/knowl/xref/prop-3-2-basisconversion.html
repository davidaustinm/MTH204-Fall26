Proposition 17.

If \(\mathcal{B}\) is a basis and \(P_\mathcal{B}\) the matrix whose columns are the basis vectors, then
\begin{equation*} \xvec = P_{\mathcal{B}} \{\xvec\}_{\mathcal{B}} \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} \{\xvec\}_{\mathcal{B}} = P_{\mathcal{B}}^{-1}\xvec \end{equation*}
