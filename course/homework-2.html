    15.

In addition, suppose that you know that for some vector \({\vec b}\) in \({\mathbb{R}}^{12}\text{,}\) \({\vec b}\) can be written as a linear combination of the vectors \({\vec v}_{1}\text{,}\) \({\vec v}_{2}\text{,}\) \(\ldots\text{,}\) \({\vec v}_{12}\) in exactly one way.
